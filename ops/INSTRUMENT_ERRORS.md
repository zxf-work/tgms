# Instrument, harness and forecasting errors

This is the full catalogue of errors we found in our own instruments, test
harnesses and forecasts while evaluating TGMS: twenty-one entries, numbered 1
to 21. The accompanying paper shows four of them (rows 1, 4, 19 and 20) in its
instrument-error table, and cites others by number (for example rows 7, 14, 15, 16 and 17); the
numbering here is the paper's numbering, so a row number means the same thing in
both places. Numbers in the table are given as the paper prints them.

Each row says what was wrong, how it was found, what it changed in the
record, the matching entry in [`failure_ledger.jsonl`](failure_ledger.jsonl)
where one exists (a dash where the defect lives in a measurement harness or a
forecast that the ledger, which holds defects in TGMS itself, does not carry),
and the public record the error touches. Where an entry's *Unresolved* note
says something could not be settled, that question stays open.

Classes: **instrument** = a measurement or check that could not see (or
mis-reported) what it claimed to; **harness or reference-side** = a fault in
the test driver or in the system we compared against, not in the instrument's
own logic; **forecasting** = a prediction frozen in a form that was wrong about
the system.
